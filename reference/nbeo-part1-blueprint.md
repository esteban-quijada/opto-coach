# NBEO Part I (ABS) — Applied Basic Science Reference

## Exam Overview

- **Format:** ~250 multiple-choice questions
- **Duration:** Full day, multiple sessions
- **Passing:** Scaled score (target ~75%+ correct, varies by form)
- **Eligibility:** Completion of required optometry coursework

## High-Yield Cross-Subject Connections

These topics appear across multiple subjects and are heavily tested:

### Glaucoma Nexus
- Anatomy: trabecular meshwork, Schlemm's canal, ciliary body
- Physiology: aqueous humor dynamics, IOP regulation
- Disease: POAG, PACG, secondary glaucomas
- Pharmacology: beta-blockers, prostaglandin analogs, CAIs, alpha-agonists, miotics
- Optics: visual field defects, nerve fiber layer patterns

### Corneal Nexus
- Anatomy: 5 layers, innervation, limbal anatomy
- Physiology: transparency, endothelial pump, deturgescence
- Disease: keratitis (bacterial, viral, fungal), dystrophies, ectasias, dry eye
- Pharmacology: antibiotics, antivirals, anti-inflammatories, cycloplegics

### Retinal Nexus
- Anatomy: 10 layers, macula, fovea, blood supply
- Physiology: phototransduction, dark/light adaptation
- Disease: DR, ARMD, detachments, vascular occlusions
- Pharmacology: anti-VEGF, steroids
- Perception: color vision, contrast sensitivity

### Pupil/Autonomic Nexus
- Anatomy: iris muscles, ciliary ganglion, sympathetic chain
- Physiology: pupillary pathways, accommodation
- Disease: Horner's, Adie's, RAPD
- Pharmacology: mydriatics, miotics, cycloplegics
- Systemic pharm: autonomic drug classification

### Cranial Nerve Nexus
- Anatomy: CN III, IV, VI (EOMs); CN V (sensation); CN VII (facial)
- Disease: palsies, neuropathies
- Systemic disease: MS, MG, diabetes, tumors

## Study Priority Matrix

Based on exam weight:

**Tier 1 — Highest yield (study first and most frequently):**
- Ocular Disease (highest weight, most cards)
- Ocular Anatomy (foundation for everything)
- Ocular Pharmacology (high clinical overlap with disease)

**Tier 2 — High yield:**
- Systemic Disease
- Systemic Pharmacology
- Ocular Physiology

**Tier 3 — Moderate yield:**
- Physiological Optics
- General Physiology
- Visual Perception

**Tier 4 — Lower yield but still tested:**
- Biochemistry, Neuroscience, Histology, Human Development, Immunology, Geometric Optics, Ocular Embryology
